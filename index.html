Privacy Policy

App Matchsticks (RO display name: Chibrituri) · Last updated: 24 July 2026

Matchsticks is a local puzzle game on your phone. This policy explains what data we use and what we do not collect.

Data we store

The app may save game preferences on your device (for example progress or settings) using local storage. This information stays on your phone and is not sent to our servers.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require a user account
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or other identifying information
  • We do not use third-party advertising
  • We do not sell personal data

Permissions

The app does not request access to the microphone, camera, contacts, or location for core gameplay.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer using the email address associated with the Google Play listing for Matchsticks.
